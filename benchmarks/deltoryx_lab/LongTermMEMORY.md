# Deltoryx v2.0 Epistemic & Legal Domain Memory

1. **Exhaustive Issue Coverage (Zero Omission Principle)**:
   - Legal grading rubrics evaluate whether **every** modified clause, added/deleted definition, changed threshold/cap/basket, modified cure/grace period, governing law/venue shift, and email-flagged concern is explicitly identified and analyzed.
   - For every issue in a markup, redline, discrepancy review, or litigation memo, structure the analysis with five explicit elements:
     1. **Section / Provision & Issue Title** (exact section numbers from both baseline and competing documents).
     2. **Baseline / Controlling Position vs. Counterparty Markup / Competing Text** (quoting exact wording and exact old vs. new numbers/percentages/days).
     3. **Playbook Standard & Partner/Associate Email Directive** (identifying Preferred Position, Acceptable Fallback, and Hard No thresholds—or noting explicitly if the Playbook is silent).
     4. **Quantitative & Financial Impact Analysis** (computing exact dollar amounts, percentages, EBITDA add-back impacts, covenant headroom/cushion, or exposure using the supplied `.xlsx` financial data).
     5. **Risk Classification & Actionable Recommendation / Counter-Proposal** (stating clearly whether to Accept, Reject, or Counter/Revise, the risk severity such as High / Medium / Low or Hard No violation, and providing concrete redline/fallback language).

2. **Cross-Document Reconciliation Patterns**:
   - **M&A / Credit / Commercial Redlines**: Cross-reference every numerical basket, cap, survival period, EBITDA add-back, financial covenant, and default trigger against the financial model (`.xlsx`), the Negotiation Playbook, and the Partner/Cover Email. Check whether counterparty comments/rationales in the markup are supported or contradicted by the financial spreadsheet.
   - **Offering Memorandum vs. Indenture / Closing Checklists**: Treat the Indenture / Controlling Agreement (`TIER_3_PRIMARY_INSTRUMENT`) as authoritative when identifying discrepancies in the Offering Memorandum (`TIER_2_COMPETING_INSTRUMENT`), and address every concern raised in the Associate/Partner Review Email (`TIER_1_DIRECTIVE_EMAIL`) plus any additional discrepancies uncovered in the full text diff.
   - **Litigation / Motion to Dismiss / Dispute Memos**: Separate pleaded allegations (`Complaint`), contractual terms (`MSA` / governing contract), extrinsic declarations/emails (`Declaration` / `.eml`), and legal standards. Evaluate each count/claim, procedural posture, extrinsic evidence admissibility on a motion to dismiss, contractual limitations of liability/notice/cure clauses, and affirmative defenses.
